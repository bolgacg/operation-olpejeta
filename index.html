MORNING ASSIGNMENT

Good morning, coordinator. Three rules before the rotors turn:

  1. Photograph the giraffe herd and the zebra herd. Fly into the green ring and hold steady; the camera fires itself.
  2. Never cross a red circle. Drone noise disturbs the animals; the closer you fly, the worse it gets. The science is on this page, one tap away.
  3. Bring the drone home to the pad before the battery dies. You have one aircraft and the accountant knows its price.
WHY THE RED CIRCLE EXISTS

"Higher altitudes corresponded to only brief vigilance by multiple individuals… at the lowest altitudes (below 40 m), sustained vigilance and short-distance relocation were more common."

"Behavioral responses appeared to vary with flight altitude and group composition, with greater disturbance recorded during dual-drone operations and in mixed-species assemblages."

Afridi, Laporte-Devylder, Maalouf, …, Schultz Lundquist et al., "Wildlife Responses to Drone Noise: A Preliminary Approach for Quantifying Disturbance", Ecology and Evolution, 2026. Field site: Ol Pejeta Conservancy — the same zebras you are photographing.
