Get Bolgaç an interview for job 4209 (engineer assistant, SDU Drone Center). The game is the
post-application attention artifact: submitted application (Monday) -> Michael Larsen email

## Bo's directives, verbatim-close, dated 21 Aug

1. DISTRIBUTION IS THE STRATEGY: "I fully don't expect Di Wu and the other professors to
   respond, because they will never try the demos. But if we send something interesting to
   Michael Larsen and ask him to get Lundquist to try it, then the guy is most likely to try
   it." Fun bar is set by MICHAEL. Homage layer aims at LUNDQUIST.
2. RADICAL HONESTY IN THE PITCH: "very openly tell them both that the reason I put so much
   effort into researching all these things and building this little game was both to showcase
   my skills and understanding of drone systems, and because I really want this job and wanted
   to make a nice impression for his consideration."
3. GAMIFY WILDOPS: "then we can literally say that we gamified his thing." Credit Guy Maalouf
   by name (MIT license honored; he is a likely future colleague).
4. REPLICA FIDELITY: "show him that we cared enough to understand the specific details of the
   drones she was working with in Kenya... exact replicas with the correct flight
   characteristics." Fleet = DJI Mavic 3E (primary/mapping), Mavic 2T (thermal), Mini 4 Pro
   (sub-250g scout); 15 m/s, ~7 km cap, BVLOS 1-2 km legs; SORA airspace geometry (flight
   geography / contingency volume / ground buffers); KAF-style clearance sector.
5. FULLY CIVILIAN: conservation story only. No defence framing anywhere in the artifact.
6. TWO LAYERS: Level 1 = Flight Control-style path-drawing arcade ("devolves into one of those
   aircraft landing type games... draw paths for drones without running out of battery and
   losing a drone"); Level 2 = the operations simulator ("game and minigame vibes, but it is
   actually a simulator... the executive panel").
7. PRE-FLIGHT PROOF: "before sending a drone on a mission there can be some sort of minigame
   to prove that the mission is safe" -> the refusal gate.
8. FLEET AWARENESS: "show other drones in the area and there are exclusion zones so we
   automatically create paths where drones won't overlap."
9. THE BOT: "make this game compatible to a bot that we build custom for it which can show
   what the actual perfect performance is on human speed... a display of how we would be able
   to manage a whole drone flight."
10. PAPER POP-UPS: "any time we are using one of his theories, a pop-up of the first page of
    the paper he wrote on that topic should show up with his title still legible, explaining
    how this game/demo is really relevant to his work."
11. JUICE: "sounds and things to make the game feel interactive... a funny robotic narrator
    voice... floating arrows telling people where to press just like the walkthrough of a
    flash game."
12. WILDLIFE MISSIONS: "herds of giraffes or lions that we need to take pictures of, or
    hunters suspected in an area that we need to send a drone and illuminate."
13. ART: 8-bit pixel safari (style B), sprite sheet v3 APPROVED 22 Aug. Craft bar: "make the
    images look pretty"; loops before done.
14. TIMEBOX: "we don't want it to be something that can take too much time." 3-4 evenings.
15. FOCUS GOVERNANCE (this paper): directives first, storyline second with per-feature
    contribution to the objective, build third.
